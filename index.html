A FEW MOMENTS TO A FINISHED VIDEO

Editor guide

  1. Bring the two halves together.

    Choose a local video and a CSV with time_ms (or time_us/time_s), thrust, and pressure columns. Set the source units in Project and the converted video units in Appearance.

  2. Keep the part that matters.

    Drag the trim handles or enter In and Out times. These refer to the original video. The Video inspector shows source frames and matching CSV rows. Set In starts at the displayed frame; Set Out includes it. Press I or O to set either edge. The start button or Home returns to the selected In point without changing synchronization.

  3. Find the ignition.

    Pause on the first visible ignition frame. Check the suggested CSV ignition time, then click “Align ignition here” or press S. Drag the CSV track or adjust its offset to refine synchronization.

  4. Give the data its place.

    In Appearance, choose Full frame + data to keep the entire video visible, with charts below and a test record beside it. In Project, add the motor model, date, logger, camera, and notes. Choose an output aspect ratio. Overlay on footage remains available. Thrust and pressure have separate labeled scales.

  5. Correct a timing mismatch, when needed.

    In Advanced · telemetry time stretch, pause at the matching end event and use “Align end here.” Set CSV end event to the burnout time if your log continues after the burn. This keeps the aligned ignition fixed. You can also enter a duration, drag the CSV right edge, or match two events. 200% spreads one logger second over two video seconds. The chart keeps the original CSV clock and impulse; exported frames label the changed timing.

  6. Measure the burn.

    In Measure telemetry, drag A and B onto the thrust rise and decay or use Detect burn. The result uses original CSV seconds. Reuse A/B as synchronization events or show the interval in the exported video.

  7. Export the finished cut.

    Choose MP4 or WebM, resolution, and quality. The overlay is rendered into every frame; original audio is retained when enabled. No upload or account is required.

Suggested ignition uses 5% of peak thrust. Verify it visually. Gaps longer than the greater of 0.5 seconds or three typical logger intervals stay blank and do not contribute to impulse. No baseline correction or smoothing is applied.
